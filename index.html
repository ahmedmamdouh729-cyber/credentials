Certification Advanced
The CMA® is the globally recognized, advanced-level professional certification for accounting and financial professionals in business. Achieving the CMA demonstrates expertise in financial planning, analysis, control, decision support, and professional ethics – skills that are in high demand on finance teams around the world.
Learn more

Certification Criteria

  • Pass the CMA exams
  • Have two continuous years of professional experience in management accounting or financial management
  • Hold a bachelor's degree from an accredited college/university or a related professional certification
  • Maintain membership in IMA
  • Abide by IMA's Statement of Ethical Professional Practice
  • 30 hours of continuing professional education are required annually to maintain CMA credential
